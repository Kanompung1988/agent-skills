## Selection and guardrails

**Terraform plan:** a read-only, 10 MB capped saved-plan JSON analyzer. Rules TP001–TP007 cover deletes, replacement ordering, drift, check failures, removed outputs and unknown outputs. Mask for_each keys; deliberately avoid dumping before/after, variables or diagnostics. `terraform show -json` itself can reveal plaintext secrets, so never publish real plan exports. Does not predict provider downtime or approve `terraform apply`.

**NetworkPolicy:** a read-only 1 MB capped safe YAML parser with NP001–NP008 checks. Explains policyTypes defaults, all-peer/all-port allow, all-namespace selector, broad CIDRs, separate OR peers and empty-direction default deny. It cannot evaluate additive policies across a cluster, CNI enforcement, NAT, or actual reachability. Scores intentionally reflect narrower semantics than the earlier broad CNI-simulation idea.

Both are original AREAZERO focused analyzers, without claims of uniqueness globally. Synthetic samples are not evidence of discovering a vulnerability in any real customer's infrastructure.
